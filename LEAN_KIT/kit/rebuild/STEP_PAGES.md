## skin-points
- A restart spot whose box holds NO source-triangle centroid is not a points spot (31br G4c): the runner releases it and says so - nothing is picked there; its tries were the lines' or the build's.
LOOK AT: SP7d_* - the original's wireframe on the source mesh, every point coloured by the face it lies on; the frames from below too.
DECIDE: which original faces KEEP their surface (a face with a point keeps its own triangles) and which are ABSORBED (no point - its neighbour takes it). NPTS ~1000 to start.
LEVERS: PTS_BOX "x0 x1 y0 y1 z0 z1 [spacing] | why" keeps a small face; PTS_DROP "... | why" drops one on purpose; FRAME_PTS 1 puts a point beside every reinsert-face edge.
GATES: a box without its reason is refused (31bq B22); a frame face without a point is ringed magenta and named; three failed builds at one SPOT lock it - only its points are picked again (the rest kept).
LIMITS: a PTS_DROP excuses FILLING (a slot closed), never REMOVAL of material - the look names a removal under a drop box (31br G3). A face the points drop is never a source surface for a blend (31br D4).
NEXT: skin-labels (MIRROR decided once) -> skin-features.

## skin-features
LOOK AT: FEAT_labels_* - every original face's id on the layout, both sides.
DECIDE: the FEATURE of each kept face ("a spine, a vane, a support" - the client: think SIMPLE): {"feature": [ids], "_why": {"feature": "why these faces are one"}}; one face per feature per side; twins never merged across the plane.
GATES: a map without its "_why" is refused; skin-rim refuses a layout that has not been through this step.
LIMITS: a merge whose member faces stand apart (a protrusion inside a flat neighbour - the keel in the underside strips) is spanned by ONE fill through the OUTER border: the protrusion is PLANED OFF
  (the agent's d8 / d9 removed the keel this way). Keep a protruding feature its own face, or give the merged face a GUIDE through the protrusion at the wires.
LIMITS 2 (31bx R6 D3 / D5): a SLOT between two features (a 0.5 mm gap fin-to-boss) is NOT closed by the skin faces - dropping the slot's wall points only hands those faces to the neighbours and the slot
  stays through the trial; close it with a gapfill job after the burrito (FAILURES 2026-10-29 17:01: a sub-mm void is sectioned first and closed by a B-rep fill). A surface that WRAPS past 180 deg
  (a cylinder's two split halves) stays TWO faces - one feature holding both halves builds an unorientable face that fails at skin-build. A relative FEATURES path is read from the folder the
  command ran in (claude_code\), then the project root, then the job folder - said (31bx R6 F6).
LIMITS 3 (31bx K35): a crease you will FILLET must be ONE line between corners - a small face that lies along it (a flat between two chamfers, a strip) belongs in its neighbours' feature;
  left apart, the crease is cut where that face's boundary meets it and skin-build REFUSES a fillet chain across such a junction (the crease turns under CREASE_TURN_MAX there: no corner, no corner
  form). Measured: two chamfers and a 1.3 mm flat between them as three features gave three blend members and a sliver no surface spans; as ONE feature, one line and one blend - the preview PASSES.
NEXT: skin-rim (--set PICS=full once - an OK is judged on the FULL set; the loop runs quick) -> judge --what layout (every frame opened) -> skin-mirror on a symmetric edit -> skin-network -> skin-frame -> skin-wires.

## skin-wires
- sharp_ok (31bs K29): `"sharp_ok": true` on a `_fillets` entry accepts its line(s) AND their kit-mirrored twins not made; a list names the master side's numbers (a twin is accepted with its master); a carried file's list is carried with its lines. Such a line is never swept and never tried by the kernel; it stays in its chain and its chain-mates end at its junctions as at an unmade member (the taper) - the preview and the build alike (31bx X76).
- A SPLIT ending at the APEX of a V-shaped (hairpin) border (the raw border turns over HAIRPIN_DEG 120 within HAIRPIN_MM 1 there) is SAID at the read and the border is DRAWN PER LEG at that end (31bw A5b, L157): each leg its own line / arc / cubic, joined at the kept apex (rung V(LINE+ARC), pale cyan in WIRE_*), so the split's end lands on the apex and the build cuts the border into two simple legs there. Before 31bw (the agent's d14 / d19) that border was ONE smooth curve: the curve rounded the apex off and the split's end snapped onto it; where the piece between the hairpin's legs is a thin lip it gets a sliver corner no surface spans ('the surface does not take its loop'; d19: 11 deg) - a wide V holds (the agent's d15 riser split at a 173 deg apex built). The warning names the apex, the corners c<n> / junctions j<n> to end on and the way out (fold the hairpin's face into the feature so the border becomes wall edges - d19b; or end where a corner survives); the PREVIEW decides, and its verdict names the hairpin for a face it fails whose split ends at one.
LOOK AT: WIRE_* (every chain numbered, hidden lines hidden) and CORNERS_*; after the run, the PREVIEW VERDICT lines (31br G1 / G2) and every FILLET ROOM line.
DECIDE: LINES - each chain straight, one arc or one cubic with its reason; "_add": GUIDE lines (the surface passes through them) and SPLIT lines (the face made two); "_fillets": the blends
  (a single "line" or a CHAIN {"lines": [...], "r"}), "buried" faces, "leg" per split end; RAILS between corners.
THE LINES FILE (31bx R6 D4): {"<chain number from WIRE_*>": {"kind": "LINE" | "ARC" | "CUBIC", "why": "...", "on": [original face ids - optional]}, ..., "_add": [{"face": <skin face>, "role": "guide" | "split",
  "kind": "LINE" | "ARC" | "CUBIC", "from": [x, y, z] | "c<n>" | "j<n>", "to": ..., "via": [[x, y, z], ...], "poles": 4..8, "trace": true, "span": "face" | [ids], "why": "..."}], "_fillets": [{"line": <n> | "lines": [<n>, ...],
  "r": <mm>, "sharp_ok": true | [<n>], "leg": "all" | <k>, "buried": [ids], "why": "..."}]} - every entry's shape is checked in the first second (31br D5), a wrong key named. A CLOSED chain (a loop) takes no kind
  decision - split it with an _add line to decide its parts; a chain re-made tighter after a CROSSING is drawn by the ladder and your decision is SET ASIDE - both are said per chain and counted in the
  DECIDED line (31bx R6 F2). A relative LINES / RAILS path is read from the folder the command ran in (claude_code\), then the project root, then the job folder - said (31bx R6 F6).
THE PIECE RULE (the agent's d10): every piece a split makes faces ONE way - list the pieces and the direction each faces before you draw it; a new surface joins the neighbour it faces like; a split
  runs border to border, so a corner where three surfaces meet must sit ON a border - if the design needs it inside a face, give the surfaces to different faces (FEATURES) or move the corner.
  AT THE WALL TOO (31bu A6, the agent's d16-d18): a piece that owns a wall-edge segment faces the way the BODY'S OWN SURFACE faces there (the body continues across the wall) - list each piece's facing AT THE WALL beside its inboard facing before you draw it; a segment facing the other way (the jog's step at the x 24 wall faced fore, the closing piece aft - a 180 deg flip, 50 min) goes to the neighbour that faces like it (FEATURES), or the split ends on the wall corner where the facing changes; a face that owns wall edges and fails to span says both facings and the turn in its SPAN FAILED line (the preview too).
LEVERS: LINE_OFF_MAX (0.5; a line further off the part is refused - decide an ARC / CUBIC, via points, or "trace": true), ADD_SNAP, HAIRPIN_DEG / HAIRPIN_MM (120 deg within 1 mm: a split's end at a hairpin apex of the raw border is said at the read and that border chain is DRAWN PER LEG, the apex kept - 31bu A5 / 31bw A5b; the preview decides); a kit-mirrored twin chain IS its master's exact mirror image, only its end points moved onto its own junctions - nothing re-fitted; an OWN-TWIN line (its mirror lies on it) is averaged with its mirror at the POLE LEVEL the same way - exact, a grown cubic's fold kept; a kind with no pole-level form keeps its drawn line when the sampled average would move it off itself more than its asymmetry, said - 31bw TB7c F5 (31bw TB7c F3: a fresh fit of a mirrored leg had left a twin 0.15 mm off its master; distToShape misreads a C0 edge by 0.47 - K-C0-DIST) - its record's dev_max is the twin's own deviation from its own raw border, dev_master the master's, `drawn_form` the form; an own-twin chain is averaged with its mirror image and drawn in the master EDGE's own kind and pole count (a V per leg, the apex reflected), dev_fit its fit's figure (31bw, TB7b / TB7c), END_MM, JMOVE_MAX, TRACE_TUBE_MM (2: a trace follows its face
  within this of the line's own plane - a crossing farther off SIDEWAYS is a gap, interpolated; the depth under the chord is free - 31br D10; "span": "face" interpolates across the whole face), ADD_VIA_TOL (0.3: a cubic through more than two via points must pass within it,
  else "poles": 5..8 or fewer points - 31br D6; never more poles than points - the via points plus the two ends: over it the fit is underdetermined and the curve swings between the points it passes; refused at the read, 31br D5b), PREFLIGHT (1: the preview runs; 0 skips it and the judge refuses the ok).
GATES: THE PREVIEW (31br G1 / G2) - this step runs the build's own ladder on the wireframe as a child: a split piece that cannot be spanned (the fold, the 80-deg turn, the balloon), a fillet
  that cannot be made (the room, a reflex junction, a corner form), a kit-made junction piece that fails - said with the build's own words and the remedy, recorded (skin\preflight.json);
  `judge --what wires --verdict ok` is REFUSED on a failed or missing preview; a fillet you accept sharp carries "sharp_ok": true on its entry. Every decided and added line is measured against
  the part; the room line is the build's ESTIMATE (the build's FILLET ROOM line is the fact). A same-network LINES file that drops decisions carries them, said (CARRY=drop drops them - 31br D8).
LIMITS (the forms the kit cannot make - lay out round them): a fillet chain ENDS where the crease's sense changes (a round inside a chain of wall-foot creases) and where the side face's corner at
  the junction is REFLEX (both members taper to the point; a member whose side face is a sliver narrower than t + 0.30 is NOT made - lower R or make room); a corner edge at a junction gets a
  vertex ball only at the chain's R with three concave creases sharing one ball, else a single blend only where its side faces have room at the junction (31br D1) - else sharp, said; a guide
  ending on a decided fillet's crease is clipped to the pass's loop (31br D2) - prefer a split; a V-shaped crease is decided per leg (a split ending on the corner); a junction where the crease runs through a far-side face boundary turning under CREASE_TURN_MAX is NO CORNER: skin-build refuses a chain across it by name - put the far side's faces in ONE feature so the crease is one line and one blend, or accept the member that lies between the no-corner junctions sharp (`"sharp_ok": [<line>]` on the chain's entry) - its chain-mates taper to those junctions (31bx K35; 31ca R5 F1; the client 11-06: a line drawn and a face made - no corner form where no corner is).
NEXT: open every WIRE_* frame (an OK is judged on the FULL set: --set PICS=full once; the loop runs quick - 31bx R6 D7) -> kit rebuild judge --what wires -> skin-build.

## skin-build
- AFTER THE BUILD (31br L1): check(True) faces named? `kit rebuild skin-look --set FACES=<ids>` FIRST (seconds) - a real defect: judge redo --face <n> (no second review) and fix the lines / points there; a tolerance-level flag or a cap face: say so. The full look and the second review refuse until every flagged face has its look.
LOOK AT: the SKIN BUILD PASS line (the count of faces failing check(True), every line left sharp and why), every FILLET ROOM line, 'LINES PASS - FAILED, FIRST THE THREE THINGS' after a failed pass.
WHAT RUNS: the face ladder (original surface -> plane -> NURBS fill -> Coons -> plate; refused when it wraps past 80 deg), every loop cleaned at the 0.02 mm construction tolerance, the sharp pass
  sewn and checked, the kernel's fillets tried, then the LINES pass as a child (the blends by offset lines, the chains with their corners, the vertex balls), ONE retry without the members its own record blames.
GATES: a self-intersection over the 0.30 mm print tolerance fails the build; a plate that balloons past its border is refused; FACE SANITY; the frame exact; the restart rule - a SPAN failure on a piece
  the agent's added lines made is a WIRES try at that face (three lock the build until the lines there change or the preview spans it - 31br G4b); a build's failure elsewhere is a POINTS try at its spot.
LEVERS (suggestions): NURBS_RIM 0.10, NURBS_WALL_RIM 0.02, CORNER_DEG, TAPER_X, THIN_MM, FIL_SMOOTH_MM, CONTACT_MERGE_MAX, FILLET_RERUN (1: the lines pass follows the kernel's failures).
LIMITS: the room levers are ONE decision with the wires (a build whose levers differ from the judged wires' is refused); the kernel's fillet fails on NURBS fills - the lines pass is the route.
STATE: a passing build saves its whole state - the points, the layout, the feature map, the wires with their records, the piece, the verdicts AND every look record with the frames it lists (31br A2) - `kit rebuild restore --tag <T>` puts it back whole (the live state kept first); a state saved before A2 brings no look record (said). the client's word on the design re-makes a judged layout: `skin-rim --supersede "<the client's words>"` (as a kit update does); a defect in a picture: judge redo --frame.
NEXT: skin-look (quick) -> judge --what look (every frame opened; every named region said as by design or a feature lost) -> skin-mirror WHAT=piece -> the TRIAL.

## skin-look
- FACES=<ids> (31br L1): THE TARGETED LOOK - only those faces' two iso views; redo / move may be judged on it, an ok may not (the quick look is the piece's verdict); LOOK_MODE=full is refused while a check(True) face of this piece has no targeted look (LOOK_FULL_OK="<why>" overrides).
- 31bs K28: a FACES id the look cannot draw (not valid, an edge tolerance over LOOK_TOL_MAX, no triangle of it in the piece mesh) is said UNSEEN with its reason and is NOT recorded as looked at - the full look stays shut on it until a targeted look draws it (LOOK_TOL_MAX raised) or LOOK_FULL_OK (recorded); a closed band (a hole's bore) is drawn along its axis and recorded like any face (31bt).
LOOK AT: SKIN_look_* - the whole piece from above and below, the hidden faces' frames, SKIN_look_vanished_NN and its _section (GREEN both bodies, YELLOW the original only, BLUE the piece only).
DECIDE: for every NAMED region, whether the original's shape shown YELLOW left the piece BY DESIGN or is a feature LOST; a frame stamped RENDER DEFECTIVE is no evidence (make the mesh right, draw again).
GATES: the judge refuses an ok when the measure of the original's faces against the piece was NOT TAKEN; a region a PTS_DROP box with its reason, a chain's "buried" list or a decided blend's strip
  accounts for stands by its record - EXCEPT MATERIAL REMOVED under a drop box (the original stands OUTSIDE the piece): a drop box excuses filling, never removal - NAMED (31br G3; d8 / d9 planed the keel off under 'the slot is closed').
LIMITS: the quick look is the loop's; the full look once; a face no camera sees is named (a slit or a pocket). `kit tool look_at` reports the share of the SUBJECT faces (the faces the box is about) in view,
  per face - a hidden subject says so: cut it (SECTION="x 0") or use the per-face frames (31br D11).
NEXT: judge --what look -> skin-mirror WHAT=piece (a symmetric edit) -> reinsert STEP=2 / 3 / 4 --set TRIAL="<why>" -> review-body -> second --what trial --assign -> judge --what trial.

## judge
WHAT A NOTE IS: what the frames SHOW, in words - each frame opened (the look gate's record); a redo / move NAMES every place (--face / --spot).
GATES: an OK on frames of an earlier artefact is refused; a REDO / MOVE stands on the OPENED picture it cites (--frame) even when the frames record is missing or of another build - a restored layout or piece (31br A2; the record says frames_record); a stamped frame is no evidence; wires ok needs the lines measured against the part AND the preview passed (31br G1 / G2); look / skin ok
  needs the original's faces measured against the piece (31bq B18); a trial ok after its two reviewers (blockers only with the client's recorded --ruling).
LIMITS: a note that states a LIMIT ("cannot be built") names the TAG of the build that tried it, or it is refused (31br I3; FAILURES 2026-10-26: three spots called unbuildable without a try). TWIN_MIRROR (1: on a symmetric edit a changed face's twin takes its MASTER's surface reflected - both sides alike by construction, 31br A3; 0 = spanned apart, said), TWIN_RIM_PLUS (0.05: the mirrored surface may miss the twin's own rim by the master's miss + this, else the twin is spanned itself, said)
